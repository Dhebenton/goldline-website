General

How involved do we need to be throughout the process?

Not as much as you might expect. You will hear from us every couple of days with updates or decisions that need your sign-off. Beyond that, we work around you.

Not as much as you might expect. Updates and approvals every couple of days. Beyond that, we work around you.

What changes can we expect as a result of your work?

The clearest shift is usually how the business is perceived before a conversation begins. Sharper positioning, stronger first impressions, and an audience that arrives already convinced.

Stronger positioning and a presence that reflects what the business is actually worth.

What does a typical project actually involve from start to finish?

It starts with understanding the business properly. From there we move through strategy, direction, and execution in sequence. Nothing gets built before the thinking is right.

Strategy first, then direction, then execution. Nothing gets built before the thinking is right.

What makes Goldline different from other web design agencies?

We do not start with design. We start with the business. Most agencies skip the thinking and go straight to the output. That is usually why the output needs replacing a year later.

We start with the business, not the design. The thinking comes first. That is what makes the work last.

Getting Started

What happens after the initial call or enquiry?

We will take the time to understand where you are and what you are trying to achieve. If it looks like a good fit, we will outline how we would approach it and what working together would involve.

We listen, ask the right questions, and outline how we would approach it if the fit is right.

How quickly can a project realistically get started?

It depends on availability at the time. We take on a small number of projects at once, so lead times vary. The best way to find out is to get in touch and we can give you an honest answer.

It depends on current availability. Get in touch and we will give you an honest answer.

How do we know if we're a good fit to work together?

The initial conversation usually makes it clear. We work best with businesses that have already built something worth representing and want a presence that does it justice.

The first conversation usually makes it clear. We work best with businesses that have earned a stronger presence.

What do you need from us before work can begin?

An honest conversation about the business. Where it is, where it is going, and what has and has not worked. The more context you give us at the start, the stronger the work will be.

An honest conversation about the business. The more context at the start, the stronger the work.
