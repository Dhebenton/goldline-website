These terms set out how we work, what we expect from each other, and how we handle the situations that occasionally need to be dealt with clearly. They exist to protect both sides and to make sure there are no surprises along the way.

Before any work begins, we agree the scope of the engagement with the client. This is set out in a proposal and covers the services to be delivered, the timeline, and the total fee.

If the client requests changes to the agreed scope after work has started, we will assess the impact on the timeline and cost and communicate this clearly before proceeding. We will not absorb scope changes silently.

3. Payment Terms

Foundation Sprint

A deposit of 20% of the total project fee is required before work begins. The remaining balance is not due until the project is complete and ready for handover. We do not ask for full payment upfront. The deposit is non-refundable except in the circumstances set out in Section 5.

Ongoing Partnership

A deposit of 20% of the total engagement fee is required before work begins. Following this, payments are made every four weeks. The deposit is discounted from the first invoice. Access to Goldline's services during the retainer period is subject to payments being kept up to date.

All invoices are due within 14 days of issue unless otherwise agreed in writing. Where payment is not received within this period, Goldline reserves the right to pause work until the balance is settled.
