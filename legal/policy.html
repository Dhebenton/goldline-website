Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data we collect when you visit goldline.agency or get in touch with us, how we use it, and what rights you have. We have written it to be clear and easy to follow.

If you have any questions, you can reach us at legal@goldline.agency.

1. Who We Are

Goldline is a growth and web design agency. It is a trading name of Hypeify.io, registered at 49 Arbroath Road, DD7 6BH. Throughout this policy, references to Goldline, we, us, or our refer to Hypeify.io operating under the Goldline name.

We act as the data controller for personal data collected through this website. Any questions relating to how your data is handled can be directed to legal@goldline.agency.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect personal data in the following ways:

Contact forms: When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message. This is used only to respond to you and is not shared or used for any other purpose.

Cookies: Our website uses cookies to function correctly and to help us understand how it is being used. Details are covered in Section 4.

Analytics: We collect anonymised data about how visitors interact with the site, including pages visited, session duration, and traffic sources. This data does not identify you personally and is used to improve the website over time.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to respond to enquiries, understand how our website performs, and make improvements to the experience it provides.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for automated decision making. Where we process your data, we do so on the basis of legitimate interest or, where required, with your explicit consent.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We use two types:

Essential cookies are required for the site to function. They cannot be disabled without affecting how the website operates.

Analytics cookies collect anonymised, aggregated data about how visitors use the site. They do not identify individuals and are used to help us understand what is and is not working.

We use Google Analytics and our own internal tracking tag. Neither stores personally identifiable information.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the site behave.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use goldline.agency. It collects anonymised data including pages viewed, session duration, and broad location data. IP addresses are anonymised before storage.

Google may process this data outside the UK. They operate under appropriate safeguards to ensure data is handled in line with UK GDPR requirements.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the browser add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Google's privacy policy is available at google.com/policies/privacy.

6. Our Own Tracking

We also run our own internal tracking on the site. This monitors performance and user journeys across goldline.agency. The data is anonymised, used only by Goldline, and is not shared with third parties.

7. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary. Enquiry data is reviewed regularly and removed once there is no longer a reason to keep it. Analytics data is retained in line with the default settings of the tools we use.

If you would like us to delete your data, contact us at legal@goldline.agency and we will action your request promptly.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data, request erasure of your data, restrict how we process it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request a portable copy of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@goldline.agency. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to raise a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Third Party Services

We use the following third party service in connection with this website:

Google Analytics: Anonymised website analytics. Privacy policy available at google.com/policies/privacy.

We do not share personal data with any other third parties unless required to do so by law.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold. No internet-based system can be guaranteed as completely secure, but we take our responsibilities here seriously. If you have any concerns, contact us at legal@goldline.agency.

11. Changes to This Policy

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold. No internet-based system can be guaranteed as completely secure, but we take our responsibilities here seriously. If you have any concerns, contact us at legal@goldline.agency.

12. Contact

For any questions or requests relating to this policy or your personal data, please contact us at legal@goldline.agency. We will always respond.
